Using the viewer

DragPan (2D) or rotate (3D; right-drag or Shift-drag pans)
WheelZoom at the cursor
ClickPin a room, opening or void in the inspector; Esc clears
1–9, 0Toggle the first ten layers in list order
[ and ]Previous and next sheet
FFit to rooms

Everything shown is read from data/out/viewer-data.js (written by export_viewer.py) on this machine; nothing is uploaded. Review checks are kept in this browser only.
